MEDICAL POLICY MP-KNEE-07
Title: Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI) of the Knee - Medical Necessity Criteria
Version: 3.0 | Effective date: 2026-01-01 | Owner: Clinical Policy Committee
Note: Synthetic training policy for demonstration only.

Section 1. Purpose
This policy defines when an MRI of the knee without contrast (CPT 73721) is considered medically necessary for adult members aged 18 and over.

Section 2. Definitions
2.1 Conservative therapy means physiotherapy, a home exercise programme, anti-inflammatory or analgesic medication, bracing, or activity modification.
2.2 Red-flag findings means suspected fracture not visible on X-ray, suspected infection, suspected tumour, a locked knee unable to extend, or acute traumatic injury with clinical signs of ligament rupture (positive Lachman test, positive pivot shift, or gross instability).

Section 3. Coverage criteria
A knee MRI is considered medically necessary when ALL of criteria 3.1, 3.2 and 3.3 are met, OR when criterion 3.4 applies.
3.1 Conservative therapy: The member has completed at least 6 weeks of documented conservative therapy within the last 6 months, without adequate improvement. Duration is measured from the start date of therapy to the date of the MRI request.
3.2 Plain X-ray: Weight-bearing plain radiographs of the affected knee were taken within 90 days before the MRI request, and the result is documented.
3.3 Clinical findings: The treating physician documents at least one objective examination finding: positive McMurray test, joint line tenderness with mechanical symptoms (catching or locking), joint effusion, or ligamentous instability.
3.4 Red-flag exception: When any red-flag finding (Section 2.2) is documented, criteria 3.1 is waived. An X-ray is still expected when fracture is suspected, but its absence does not delay the request.

Section 4. Repeat imaging
4.1 A repeat knee MRI within 12 months of a prior knee MRI is not medically necessary unless there is a documented new injury, a significant change in clinical findings, or planned surgery requiring updated imaging.

Section 5. Not medically necessary
5.1 Generalised knee pain without any objective finding listed in criterion 3.3.
5.2 Known advanced osteoarthritis (Kellgren-Lawrence grade 3 or 4 on X-ray) when the MRI result would not change management, unless a red-flag finding is present.
5.3 Screening or routine follow-up without new symptoms.

Section 6. Required documentation
The request must include: member age, symptom onset date, conservative therapy type with start and end dates, X-ray date and result, examination findings, prior MRI history, and the requesting physician's rationale.

Section 7. Review process
Automated or AI-assisted tools may summarise evidence but must not issue coverage decisions. Every determination is made by a licensed clinical reviewer.
